The problem

Most music work leaves no usable record.

Much of the UK's music workforce is self-employed. Income arrives from teaching, performing, royalties, sales, rentals and repairs, and is pieced together months later from bank statements, messages and memory.

Today

WhatsApp→Bank transfer→Spreadsheet→Accountant→Mortgage application

The context is lost at each step. By the time anyone asks for proof, it has to be reconstructed.

With Vedsora

Vedsora→Record→Confirmation→Classification→Evidence

The record is made when the work happens, and confirmed by the person who paid.

Why now

Digital tax reporting is reaching self-employed musicians.

Making Tax Digital for Income Tax is being phased in by income level. More musicians will need digital records kept through the year, when many still rely on a year-end reconstruction.

Vedsora is a technology platform that makes the underlying records more structured, explainable and usable. It does not give tax advice and does not replace an accountant. Support for quarterly MTD workflows is planned for once the recognised-software milestone is achieved.

A general description of each category, not of any single product.

Accounting and MTD tools

They understand money and tax, but generally see a transaction after its original context has gone.

Music platforms and tutoring marketplaces

They are present in the activity, but do not provide the same tax logic, evidence layer and cross-activity record.

General marketplaces

They provide buyers and sellers, but are not designed around ongoing instrument ownership, repair, rental and professional records.

Income-verification providers

They verify records that already exist. Vedsora is designed to create the underlying evidence.

What Vedsora is

The attested ledger of music work.

Every lesson, engagement, sale, rental and repair is agreed, confirmed by the person who paid, classified for tax with the rule that applied, reconciled to what is declared, and turned into evidence the musician owns and can share with anyone they choose.

Not another music marketplace. Not another accounting app.

The record layer

Four mechanisms turn activity into evidence.

Classification at source

Transactions are classified by their appropriate UK tax treatment when the activity happens.

Off-platform capture

Cash engagements, direct transfers, royalty statements, examination fees and other outside activity can be entered, uploaded or captured, then classified.

Counterparty attestation

The person who paid or took part confirms it happened. A student confirms a lesson. A buyer confirms receipt. An owner confirms a repair.

Live tax position

Designed to support a live tax position, and future quarterly MTD workflows once the recognised-software milestone is achieved.

Innovation

Records that are confirmed by someone other than the person who benefits.

The central innovation is counterparty-attested income and instrument records, generated at the point of work, across the activities a musician undertakes.

Foundation 1 of 3

Presence at the point of income creation

Teaching, performing, selling, renting and repair are captured where the work actually happens.

Foundation 2 of 3

Classification by financial meaning

The platform does more than attach a category. It captures the relevant UK tax treatment and the rule behind it.

Foundation 3 of 3

Counterparty confirmation

The party who paid or took part confirms the activity occurred. A self-declared statement becomes a stronger evidence trail.

Where AI fits

AI assists with selected tasks: practice planning, matching, extraction, search and content tagging. It is not the product. The durable product is the record, the classification, the attestation, the instrument data and the network.

About

Music work, on the record.

Vedsora is a UK technology platform being built by IMS Empire Ltd for musicians, teachers, performers, organisers and the institutions that work with them.

Mission

Give every musician a record of their work that they own and others can trust.

Vision

Music work creates a usable record at the moment it happens, in any tradition and, in time, in any country.

UK relevance

Designed around the UK.

Vedsora is shaped by how music work is done, taxed and evidenced in the UK, and is being built with UK-based software development and infrastructure.

  • Self-employed and freelance music work
  • Making Tax Digital for Income Tax
  • Financial inclusion and professional evidence
  • Specialist instrument preservation
  • The creative industries and music education
  • Music Hubs
  • UK employment
Business model

Vedsora earns from activity, tools and trusted records.

Activity

Commission on lessons, marketplace sales, rentals and repairs, with a share of course and content sales and event services.

Tools

Subscriptions for learners, teacher tools and the record layer, and supplier storefronts or premium listings.

Trusted records

Evidence products such as Proof of Income and Proof of Practice, alongside promoted placement.

Institutions

Services for academies, Music Hubs, unions, associations and other institutions.

Potential revenue lines. Pricing has not been published.
