What Vedsora does

Vedsora keeps a record of work for self-employed musicians: who engaged you, what for, how much, and whether the payer has confirmed it. It does not give tax advice and it does not move money.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, a password (stored only in scrambled, hashed form; we cannot read it), your name and, if you give it, your postcode.
  • Your records: dates, descriptions of work, amounts, the names of people and organisations who paid you, and where an agreement was made.
  • Bank payments, if you choose to add them: the date, amount and reference of money coming in, from a bank statement file you choose to upload. We never ask for your bank login, and money going out is not saved.
  • Payer answers: when a payer opens a confirmation link, their answer (yes, a different amount, or no) and the time. We do not ask the payer for an account.
  • Technical: network address for a short time, to limit abuse of the confirmation links.

Why, and our lawful basis

  • To provide the service you asked for (contract).
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse (legitimate interests).
  • For people who paid a musician: to record your answer to a confirmation request (legitimate interests; answering is optional).

We do not sell personal data, show advertising, or use your data to train AI models.

People who paid you

You enter the names of people who paid you, and may send them a confirmation link. If you are a payer and want to know what is held about you or want it removed, email us.

Who we share data with

  • Supabase (database and sign-in; data stored in London, UK).
  • Resend (only to send account emails such as password resets).
  • Our website host (to serve this website and the confirmation page).

Your accountant or anyone else receives your record only if you send it to them yourself.

How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your records and confirmations are deleted immediately from the live service, and from any backups within 30 days. During our pilot we review whether we still need any data at the end of the pilot.

Deleting your account

In the app: Account, then Delete my account. Or see how to delete your account.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, correction, deletion, restriction, or to object, by emailing us. You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
