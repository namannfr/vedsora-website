Common questions

I forgot my password. Tap "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen and check your email, including the spam folder.

What do the grades mean? A: the agreement, the payer's confirmation and the bank payment all agree. B: the payer's confirmation agrees with one other source. C: no usable payer confirmation yet; the income counts in your totals but is not shown as evidence.

Can Vedsora move my money? No. Vedsora never asks for your bank login. You can upload a bank statement file, and only money coming in is used.

Does Vedsora give tax advice? No. It keeps records you can pass to your accountant.
