Delete your Vedsora account

Vedsora is provided by Vedsora Limited.

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Vedsora and sign in.
  2. Tap Account at the bottom right.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account, then confirm.

Without the app

Email info@vedsora.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my Vedsora account". We will confirm and delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your account, profile, all income records, payer confirmations and organiser statements you issued. Nothing is kept after deletion. Copies you have already sent to other people (for example a PDF sent to your accountant) are not affected.
